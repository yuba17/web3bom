- LayerZero $15M, Wormhole $10M+, NEAR Intents
- Attack surface requires multi-chain reasoning
- 500-1K hunters (moderate competition)
- IoTeX $4.4M exploit in 2026 proves still vulnerable

## Secondary: ZK Circuits ($250K-$1.6M payouts)
- ZKsync $1.6M, Aztec $450K, DeGate $1.1M

## Tertiary: New L2/L3 Launches (time-sensitive)
- First 3-6 months after launch = low competition window
- Monitor: Aztec, Citrea, new OP Stack/Orbit chains
- Move fast, be first
